What is running

The page grows — the trigger moves with it

Content that arrives late — a list loaded after the page, an image, an accordion — moves everything below it. ScrollTrigger measures its start and end on load and resize only; the connector refreshes them when the page changes height. The coloured lines on the right are ScrollTrigger's own markers: grow the page and watch them follow the card — or, without the connector, stay where they were.

A card that widens as it scrolls through

scrub: true — tied to the scroll itself: scroll back, it narrows. With Lenis the scroll glides, and the card glides with it.
