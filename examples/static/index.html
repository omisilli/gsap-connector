The page grows — the trigger moves with it

Content that arrives late — a list loaded after the page, an image, an accordion — moves everything below it. ScrollTrigger measures its start and end on load and resize only; the connector refreshes them when the page changes height. The coloured lines on the right are ScrollTrigger's own markers: grow the page and watch them follow the card — or, without the connector, stay where they were.

Pinned, with or without Lenis

The section above stood still while its strip slid across — pin: true, scrubbed by the scroll. Pinning adds height to the page; the connector refreshes the triggers after it.

A reload, or a link with a #, lands in the right place

Reload the page down here: you are where you were. With Lenis, this page makes its triggers only after Lenis has loaded — after the page itself — so the pin adds its height to a page the browser has already measured, and the scroll would come back short by that height. The connector keeps the scroll for the tab and puts it back once the triggers are refreshed.

An address with a # goes straight to its element — a link shared to the recipe below opens on the recipe, not a pin's height above it. Open it in a new tab; then the same without the connector, and see where it lands. A hidden element (a closed tab) is left to the page's own anchor code; restoreScroll: false turns all of this off.

Recipe — a word that opens onto a picture

No package of its own: plain GSAP, a few lines below. The picture shows only through the letters; as the scroll goes on the word comes at you from inside the U and leaves the screen, the picture opening out of the letter — and the moment the picture is whole, the page scrolls on. Every time: scroll back, and it closes again. Only the first time: once open, it stays open and shrinks to one screen — no stretch to wait through, up or down, and a reload keeps it so — data-pg-gsap-connector-reveal="once" on the section.

How fast: the scroll the scene takes, --pg-gsap-connector-reveal-length in the CSS — 225svh by default, a little over two screens. Shorter is faster.

Recipe — a video that grows as it passes

No package of its own either: plain GSAP. A 16:9 video in the page; as it comes to the middle of the screen it grows, and as the page goes on it shrinks back to its place. Nothing is pinned — the page scrolls on all the while. It plays while it is in sight and stops when it is not — unless you stopped it: then it waits for you. Its buttons, at the bottom right: sound, play / pause and full screen.

Or it waits for play from the start — data-pg-gsap-connector-grow-autoplay="false" on the section; once played, it plays in sight like the other. A picture to show while it waits is the video's own poster.

Plays in sight Waits for play

The light pink is the area kept for it, one screen: nothing around moves or is covered. What it grows to is a box in its middle — the whole area, or less: CSS, --pg-gsap-connector-grow-to-width and -to-height. Its size at rest, --pg-gsap-connector-grow-width, and its corners, --pg-gsap-connector-grow-radius.
